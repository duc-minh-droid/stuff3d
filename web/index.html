Every number and pixel below comes from stuff3d --trace: the C++ renderer ran a scripted 13 s session and recorded what it drew each frame.

close to camera far away edge cut at z = near tracked vertex (beak tip)

The renderer has no depth buffer and no matrices. Each frame it pushes all 324 vertices through five hand-written steps (right), divides by z, and draws the triangle edges as lines. Edges that cross the near plane (z = 0.1) are cut before the divide; without that cut, points behind the camera would flip to the opposite side of the screen. Walk into the penguin around the 7 s mark to see it happen.
